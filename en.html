Frequently asked questions

What do I need?

An Android phone and a Plex server. Seerr, Tautulli, Radarr and Sonarr are welcome: each one adds its own features. As a guest, all you need is your host’s invitation.

Is it free?

Yes, both the test and the app: no ads, no subscription.

What does the app collect?

Nothing for us. Rainarr talks to your servers, and to TMDB for detail pages and search. The details are in the privacy policy.

What about iPhone?

Android first. iPhone will come after the launch.

How many spots?

Fifty. Once the panel is full, sign-ups close.
