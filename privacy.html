Rainarr privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · Version française (the French version prevails) · Legal notice

In short

  • Rainarr is a mobile app that controls your own media servers (Plex, Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, Tautulli, Seerr, Maintainerr, torrent client).
  • Rainarr has no server: the publisher only receives what you choose to write to them, such as a problem report.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reports sent.
  • Your credentials stay on your device, encrypted.

Who publishes Rainarr

Rainarr is published on a non-professional basis by an individual residing in France, under the name Rainarr. They are the controller of the processing described under “What the publisher processes”; their identity has been provided to the website host (see the legal notice). For any question about your data: support@rainarr.app.

Everything that happens on your device, and between your device and your servers, happens at your initiative and under your control: the publisher has no access to it.

What is sent, and to whom

To your servers. Rainarr sends each server you configure what it needs to control it: its key or your credentials, your searches, and the movies and series you add, request or delete. You choose these servers; the publisher has no access to them. Credentials are only sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), or to your local network.

In the guided setup, when you ask for it: Rainarr looks for your services on your local network by querying its devices on the usual ports of this software, with a simple request that recognizes them, without credentials. It can also use your Plex sign-in to open a session on your Seerr server and read your services' keys there. If you give it a service's username and password, it uses them to read its key and then forgets them; only your torrent client's are kept, as in its settings.

In the background, if you turn on notifications: about every fifteen minutes, Rainarr asks those same servers what changed — your requests, new requests, finished downloads, services that stopped responding. Nothing else is contacted, and it all stays off until you turn on a notification.

If you report a problem: Rainarr prepares a report — version, phone model, language and recent errors, with no address or credential — and shows it to you in full. It only leaves if you share it, and only to where you choose to send it.

If you join a server as a guest

You sign in with your Plex account, on Plex's website. Rainarr then receives a token for your account and passes it to the Seerr server you are joining to open your session: that server thereby gets access to your Plex account, so only join one run by someone you trust. Rainarr keeps this token, encrypted, on your device for “My server”: with it, Rainarr asks Plex which servers are shared with you, reads on your host's server what they share with you (what you're watching, their latest additions, titles leaving soon) and, when you tap “Keep” or “My watchlist”, adds the title to your Plex watchlist. To do this, when you open a movie or show page, Rainarr asks Plex which title in its catalog matches it, using its TMDB identifier. It leaves with the server when you leave it. If that server allows new Plex users to sign in, this first sign-in creates an account in your name there, based on your Plex account; otherwise it is refused.

That account belongs to the Seerr server, not to Rainarr. When you leave a server, Rainarr closes your session and erases what it kept about it on your device, but your account stays on the server: only the person who runs it can delete it, and you should ask them to.

Transfers outside the European Union

TMDB, Plex, Google (YouTube), GitHub and Cloudflare are based in, or process data in, the United States. Depending on each company, these transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses; their policies, linked above, say which.

How long, and how to erase

  • Your data stays on your device until you erase it.
  • Settings → Erase all data removes everything Rainarr keeps on the device and closes your guest sessions. Nothing is deleted on your servers.
  • Uninstalling the app also erases its data.
  • Your exchanges with support are deleted 3 years after the last message, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to set instructions for what happens to it after your death. What Rainarr keeps on your device can be viewed and erased directly in the app. For what the publisher holds (your exchanges with support), write to support@rainarr.app: you will get an answer within one month. For data held by your servers or by third-party services, contact their administrator or publisher. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Minors

Rainarr is intended for people aged 18 and over.
